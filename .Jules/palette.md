## 2024-04-13 - [Focus Visible and ARIA Labels]
**Learning:** Custom interactive elements lacked explicit `:focus-visible` states and icon-only buttons lacked `aria-label`s, making keyboard navigation and screen reader usage difficult.
**Action:** Always ensure that explicit `:focus-visible` states are defined for custom interactive elements (buttons, inputs) and that icon-only buttons have descriptive `aria-label`s to improve overall accessibility for keyboard and screen reader users.
